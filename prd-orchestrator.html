Design thesis

Automation with evidence, judgment, and release control

Evidence first

Ground every requirement

Stable citation and requirement identifiers preserve the origin and disposition of every approved product claim.

Human accountable

Keep decisions visible

Conflicts and gaps are surfaced for approval; the system does not silently manufacture business judgment.

Fail closed

Release only reconciled work

Deterministic controls, semantic evaluation, and the Agreement Gate protect the final export boundary.

Business problem

Manual documentation loses context between evidence and delivery

The operational risk is not merely slow writing; it is untraceable interpretation.

Fragmentation

Evidence lives in many formats

Briefs, transcripts, notes, clarifications, and templates must be reconciled before product work can begin.

Ambiguity

Missing detail becomes accidental invention

Traditional drafting can turn uncertainty into confident prose without preserving what remains unresolved.

Traceability

Delivery loses its source lineage

Stories and acceptance criteria are difficult to defend when the chain back to stakeholder evidence is broken.

Alignment

Contradictions surface too late

Conflicting goals, dates, scope, and technical constraints create rework after planning has started.

Consistency

Decomposition varies by author

PRDs, features, stories, and acceptance criteria drift in structure and completeness across teams.

Governance

Generation can outrun approval

Without a release boundary, plausible-looking outputs can be distributed before human decisions are recorded.

Solution approach

Each failure mode maps to a governed system capability

Ground

Normalize evidence, hash current-run files, and assign exact citation identifiers.

Analyze

Extract requirements and expose gaps, conflicts, dependencies, and clarification needs.

Approve

Record signed decisions and constrain generation to the approved scope.

Validate

Reconcile sets, score semantic quality, and fail closed before delivery.

System architecture

One run identity connects source, decisions, artifacts, and evaluation

A sequential topology keeps handoffs inspectable and makes rollback practical.

Specialized roles

Separation of duties improves control and diagnosability

Requirement intelligence

Preserves exact evidence and distinguishes requirements from risks, decisions, and open questions.

Gap intelligence

Finds contradictions and missing decisions without resolving them autonomously.

Document generation

Builds the PRD and delivery structure only from approved scope and source identifiers.

Assurance

Uses deterministic reconciliation and semantic judges to protect the export boundary.

Governance & evaluation

Quality controls target different failure modes

Code checks enforce contracts; semantic judges test whether the meaning remains faithful and reasonable.

Grounding

Exact evidence references

Current-run source segments receive stable citation IDs that propagate through requirements and outputs.

Decision boundary

Human approval is explicit

Contradictions, scope decisions, and unresolved questions remain visible until an accountable owner approves them.

Release control

Agreement Gate fails closed

Export requires reconciled citation, PRD, story, and acceptance-criteria sets with no orphan delivery items.

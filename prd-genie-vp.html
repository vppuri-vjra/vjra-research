Interpretation

This dashboard presents a fresh ten-case S2 regression. Google Drive supplies the approved source fixtures and independent expectations; the workflow removes prerecorded actual outputs, dynamically invokes the S2 Requirement Extractor ten times, validates every emitted evidence quote against its case input, and fails closed unless all ten cases and trace references pass.
